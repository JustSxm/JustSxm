### 👨‍💻 Projects I'm proud of
- **[JustSxm/Deals-Scraper](https://github.com/JustSxm/Deals-Scraper)** - Multi-platform marketplace scraper aggregating deals across Facebook Marketplace, Kijiji, and eBay
- **[JustSxm/SpotifyMigrator](https://github.com/JustSxm/SpotifyMigrator)** - Automated C#/.NET tool to seamlessly migrate playlists and liked songs between Spotify accounts while preserving exact track order

- **[JustSxm/SmartPlant](https://github.com/JustSxm/SmartPlant)** - IoT-enabled plant monitoring system tracking soil moisture and environmental metrics
- **[JustSxm/CashFlow](https://github.com/JustSxm/CashFlow)** - Personal finance and cash flow tracking tool designed to monitor income, expenses, and budgeting
